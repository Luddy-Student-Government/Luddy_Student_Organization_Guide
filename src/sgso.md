# Self-Governed Student Organizations (SGSO) and BUY.IU

As of Fall 2026, Luddy organizations are transitioning from University Student Organizations (USOs) to Self-Governed Student Organizations (SGSOs). Information about the transition is posted in the [Student Org Teams](student-org-teams.md).

## Registering as a Supplier in BUY.IU
Your organization must be registered as a supplier in BUY.IU to receive any leftover Luddy funds. This is the final step of the transition. **Payment cannot be made until your registration is submitted.**

> [!WARNING]
> Only provide tax and payment information for the SGSO you are registering. **Do not enter personal tax or payment information.** If you are not an SGSO, stop and notify [helpmeiu@iu.edu](mailto:helpmeiu@iu.edu).

### Before You Start
You will need:
- Your SGSO's legal name and Employer Identification Number (EIN), as shown on the SS-4 form the IRS provides when you apply for an EIN
- A bank account in the SGSO's legal name, for ACH/Direct Deposit
- A Windows computer if possible. BUY.IU works best on non-mobile Windows devices.

### Getting the Invitation
The registration invite comes from buyiu.noreply@iu.edu to your organization's email. Check your spam folder if you do not see it. The invite **cannot be forwarded**: logging in through a forwarded invite results in an error. If the invite should go to a different address, ask [helpmeiu@iu.edu](mailto:helpmeiu@iu.edu) to resend it.

When you open the portal, you should see a BUY.IU watermark and/or "Welcome to Indiana University..." If you do not, you are not in the correct registration portal.

### Registration Checklist
Fields marked with a star [★] are required. Sections marked with an alert symbol [⚠] are incomplete, and sections with a green checkmark [✔] are complete. The Insurance and Supplier Information fields are pre-completed.

1. **Welcome:** Enter your SGSO's legal company name as provided to the IRS.
2. **Company Overview:**
   - DBA: any other names your SGSO goes by (optional)
   - Country of Origin: *United States*
   - Legal Structure: *Other*
   - US Tax ID Number: your SGSO's EIN
3. **Business Details:** Enter commodity code `PRO36` in the Commodity Codes section of Products and Services.
4. **Addresses:** Label the address with the name of your SGSO and complete all fields, including phone number.
5. **Contacts:** Enter at least one contact. This should be your group's contact information.
6. **Payment Information:** SGSOs are required to use an ACH/Direct Deposit payment method.
   - Account Holder Name must match the SGSO's legal name. **Do not provide personal banking details.**
   - Payment Title: a description of the payment type (e.g. "PNC Bank ACH")
   - Country: *United States*; Currency: *USD*
   - Electronic Remittance Email: this email will receive payment notifications
7. **Tax Information:** A W-9 is required. Click **Add Tax Document** → **W-9**, select **Download Pre-populated Tax Document**, review it, save, then upload it. On the W-9, enter the SGSO's EIN (not a Social Security number) and sign and date the certification. A blank W-9 is available from the [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-w-9).
8. **Certify & Submit:** Once every section shows a green checkmark, Certify & Submit your registration. If you see an alert symbol, click that section and complete the missing information.

You will receive a notification when your profile is approved. This does **not** mean payment has been issued. For questions about payments, contact the [Luddy Student Engagement Office](contact.md).
