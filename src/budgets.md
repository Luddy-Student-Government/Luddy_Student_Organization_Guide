# Budgets and Submitting Budgets
**All Luddy USOs are sponsored through the Office of Student Engagement and Success. If you are an SGSO asking for funding, also submit a budget**  
Student organizations must submit their budgets for approval each semester. Deadlines and submission details are shared via email or during Presidents Meetings, hosted by the Office of Student Engagement and Success. These can be submitted by [submitting to the LSG form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/SiihyFkYn4).

P-Card requests cannot be processed until your budget is approved.

## Planning Your Budget
- **Food:** The office cannot cover food at every event. Explore other funding for food, such as a Luddy department, an outside sponsor, or the [IU Funding Board](funding.md#iu-funding-board).
- **Supplies and marketing/printing** are more easily supported.
- **Sponsors:** If an event is working with a sponsor, include what the sponsor has agreed to cover. See [Corporate and Outside Sponsorship](funding.md#corporate-and-outside-sponsorship).

## If Your Budget Is Denied 
1. Make the revisions requested in your denial email.
2. Then schedule a meeting with the Office of Student Engagement and Success. During the meeting, you will review the changes you made, discuss any remaining questions, and work toward finalizing your budget.
