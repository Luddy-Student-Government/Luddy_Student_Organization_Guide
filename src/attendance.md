# Tracking Event Attendance

Attendance must be tracked at every event. **P-Card requests will not be approved without tracked attendance.**

- Use the same attendance QR code as last year for every event. The QR code PNG is in the Clubs & Orgs channel of the [Student Org Teams](student-org-teams.md), under the Shared tab.
- If you used the P-Card for a purchase, make sure attendees scan the QR code at that event.
- If you didn't collect attendance with the QR code, email your attendance to [lsg@iu.edu](mailto:lsg@iu.edu) and cc [marganey@iu.edu](mailto:marganey@iu.edu) and [jaquest@iu.edu](mailto:jaquest@iu.edu).
