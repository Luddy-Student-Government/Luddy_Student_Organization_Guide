# Tracking Event Attendance

Attendance must be tracked at every event. **P-Card requests will not be approved without tracked attendance.**

- **Use the same attendance QR code as last year for every event. The QR code PNG is in the Clubs & Orgs channel of the [Student Org Teams](student-org-teams.md), under the Shared tab.**

- **Required for P-Card events:** For any event that used the P-Card, email your attendance to [lsg@iu.edu](mailto:lsg@iu.edu) and cc [marganey@iu.edu](mailto:marganey@iu.edu) and [jaquest@iu.edu](mailto:jaquest@iu.edu).
- **Optional for other events:** If you would like to keep your attendance on record for future P-Card requests, send it to the same addresses.
