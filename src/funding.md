# Student Organizations Funding

## Luddy Budgets
Each semester, organizations submit a budget to the Office of Student Engagement and Success. See [Budgets and Submitting Budgets](budgets.md). Spending from an approved budget is done with a [P-Card](pcard.md). Reimbursements should be an emergency option.

## IU Funding Board
The IU Funding Board provides additional university funding to student organizations. Applications are submitted through the Finance Tool on [BeInvolved](https://beinvolved.indiana.edu/).

## Corporate and Outside Sponsorship
Communication between sponsors and students will be mediated through the Office of Student Engagement and Success. Find the sponsorship packet within our [Teams Channel](student-org-teams.md), which has information about sponsorship opportunities.

When an event is working with a sponsor, such as a company or organization, send the office documentation showing the amount the sponsor has agreed to cover for the event. Provide this as soon as the sponsorship arrangement is confirmed.

## Required Documents
Other than budgets, **new student organizations need to submit more documents to have access to funding.** These documents include: 
- Constitution
   : This is the document you drafted when registering your student org. For existing orgs, resubmit one when you change your constitution. Constitutions should be submitted in the Luddy Student Government Teams channel under: Files → Student Org Constitutions. 
- Logos
  : This is your student org official logo. For existing orgs, resubmit your logo when you change it. Logos should be submitted in the Luddy Student Government Teams channel under: Files → Student Org Logos. 
- Member list
  : This is an official roster of club officers. Member lists should be submitted in the Luddy Student Government Teams channel under: Clubs and Organizations → Files → Member List.
