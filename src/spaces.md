# Reserving Spaces

## Luddy Spaces (Luddy Hall, Luddy AI Building, and the Community Center)
As of Fall 2026, all Luddy spaces, including the Community Center, are booked through [25Live](https://one.iu.edu/task/iu/25livepro). General use reservations in Luddy Hall, Luddy AI, and Myles Brand Hall are limited to a total of 8 hours per week per person.

### Booking the Community Center
1. In 25Live, switch the **Select Object** dropdown to **Locations** and search `BLIF`.
2. Find **BLIF 1006** (Luddy Hall - Community Center - 1006) in the list of locations.
3. Open the **Event Form** and switch **Select Form Configuration** to **BL Luddy Public Request Form**.
4. Fill out the event details:
   - **Event Name**
   - **Luddy Affiliation**: click the dropdown, start typing "Luddy", and select *Luddy Student (BL)*
   - **Expected Head Count**
   - **Event Description**: 2-3 sentences describing your event
   - **Date and Time**
5. For a recurring meeting, click **Repeating Pattern**, choose how often it repeats (e.g. *Weekly*), check the day of the week, set the **Repeats through** date, and click **Select Pattern**.
6. Select the Community Center under **Locations** and click **Save**.

> [!NOTE]
> Ignore any conflicts that come up. Your request will be entered into the system as tentative, and you can still submit it.

If your event is in the Community Center, you must also submit a TV-format poster with your P-Card request. See [Flyers](flyers.md#event-flyers-and-tv-posters).

### Community Center Cleanup Expectations
To help ensure that we can continue offering the Community Center space:
- No food should be left behind in the Community Center.
- All trash must be collected and disposed of before leaving.
- Tables, chairs, and any other items used should be returned to their original locations and wiped down.
- The Cleanup Coordinator should do a final walkthrough before everyone leaves to ensure the space is clean and ready for the next group.
- Everyone attending is expected to assist with cleanup as needed.

Do not take items from the Clubs & Orgs cabinet that aren't yours. If you are unsure, ask.

## Classrooms, the IMU, and Other Campus Spaces
As of Fall 2026, space outside of Luddy is requested through your organization's Event Tool on [BeInvolved](https://beinvolved.indiana.edu/):
1. Create an event request from your organization's page.
2. Under **Time & Place**, choose **Add Reservation Request**.
3. Select **Browse Available Rooms** to search spaces with their availability filled in automatically, or choose **Classroom To Be Determined** or **IMU To Be Determined** to request a space that isn't listed.

The Student Life website's space request pages are being moved under the Indiana Memorial Union section, so older links may not work. For help, or to schedule a tutorial for your organization, contact McKenna Myers, Senior Assistant Director of Student Organizations, at [mckmyers@iu.edu](mailto:mckmyers@iu.edu) or (812) 855-6315.

For any other space, reach out to the [Luddy Student Engagement Office](contact.md).
